Custom Awards Project

Major League Baseball Sustainability Awards, Made Using Diverted Stadium Materials

“Working with Ray is a pleasure. He brings a high level of creativity to every project and that has helped elevate the MLB Green awards. He delivers terrific results and we always enjoy collaborating with him.”

MLB Stadium Operations Team

The Challenge

In 2023, Major League Baseball set out to expand its sustainability recognition, growing from a single league-wide honor — the Green Glove Award — to multiple distinct awards spanning different areas of sustainability. WM introduced us to MLB, and together we developed the trophies to reflect the league's commitment to innovation and environmental stewardship.

The Diverted Materials Used

Working with MLB spring training stadiums, we diverted replica helmets originally used for nachos and Dippin' Dots service, all made from polypropylene (PP), along with a large volume of signature PP stadium cups. We also sourced replaced stadium seating made from high-density polyethylene (HDPE), giving these discarded materials a second life as award-worthy components.

The Outcome

Through shredding, melting, machining, and assembly, the diverted materials became custom sustainability trophies crafted in each team's colors. They now are symbols of their efforts and values. A behind-the-scenes video captured the full journey, from material recovery to final presentation. At the 2026 MLB All-Star Game in Philadelphia, this year's award recipients were celebrated with their trophies and video on display.
